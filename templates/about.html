About the Hackathon

Welcome to our Community Hackathon, where students, developers, designers and innovators come together to solve real-world social problems.

Participants will work in teams to develop creative, technology-driven solutions that can create a positive impact on society.

Goal: Turn innovative ideas into meaningful solutions.

Event Details

Theme: Social Innovation

Mode: Offline Hackathon

Team Size: 2 - 4 Members

Duration: 24 Hours

Participants: College Students

Event Schedule

09:00 AM — Registration

Participants check in and complete registration.

10:00 AM — Opening Ceremony

Introduction, rules and problem statements.

11:00 AM — Hackathon Begins

Teams start developing their solutions.

06:00 PM — Mentor Review

Teams receive feedback from mentors.

Next Day — Final Presentation

Teams present their projects to the judges.

Prize Distribution

Winners are announced and awarded.

Hackathon Rules

  • Each team must have 2 to 4 members.
  • Participants must register before the deadline.
  • The project must be related to the given theme.
  • Teams must develop their project during the hackathon.
  • Previously completed projects should not be submitted.
  • Teams must present their solution to the judges.
  • The decision of the judges will be final.
